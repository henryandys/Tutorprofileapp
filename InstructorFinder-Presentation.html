Community Growth
Why the map makes people want to sign up

The map isn't just a feature — it's an emotional hook. The moment someone sees instructors pinned on streets they recognise, the platform stops feeling like a website and starts feeling like their neighbourhood.

📍

"That's my street!"

Seeing price-tagged pins on real local streets triggers an immediate sense of proximity and relevance — a stranger becomes a neighbour. That personal recognition is the strongest conversion trigger the platform has.

🏅

Verified gold pins stand out

Admin-verified instructors display a gold pin that stands out visually on the map. Students learn that the badge signals quality — and instructors see getting verified as worth pursuing to appear more prominently.

👥

Group session pins create urgency

Purple group lesson pins show a class near you with "4 spots left." That scarcity — visible on the map before you even click — pulls people directly to sign up and enrol before the seat disappears.

💰

Price transparency removes hesitation

Showing $45 or $80 right on the pin lets visitors compare options without opening every profile. Removing that friction means browsers become bookers faster.

🔎

Location search lowers the barrier

Anyone who types their city or zip instantly sees a map centred on their area. That zero-friction localisation makes the first visit feel personal and worth returning to.

The Map — Instructor Perspective
Instructors sign up because the map shows the opportunity
What instructors see
🗺️

Their neighbourhood has demand

When a new instructor browses the map before signing up, they see students searching in their area with no available match. That visible gap is a direct pitch: "your community needs you."

⭐

Verified badge = more bookings

Gold pins attract more clicks than blue ones — visible right there on the map. Instructors who understand this are motivated to complete verification to gain that visual advantage in search.

👥

Group lessons multiply earnings

Instructors can create group sessions pinned to a real location. One class of 6 students at $25/seat earns $150 for a single session — visible to the whole map audience at once.

What makes instructors stay
📊

Dashboard proves ROI immediately

The Teaching tab shows upcoming sessions, pending requests, student count, and average rating — all above the fold. Within the first week a new instructor can see their traction growing.

💬

Messaging keeps students close

Real-time chat means instructors can answer questions and close bookings without exchanging personal contact details — reducing the friction that causes drop-off between first contact and paid session.

🎯

Student milestones create loyalty

When an instructor marks a student's learning milestone, it creates a tangible record of shared progress — the kind of relationship that keeps both parties on the platform long-term.

Community Engagement
Why the Requested Courses page turns visitors into members

A ranked leaderboard of what the community wants most isn't just data — it's a public declaration of local need. It makes every visitor feel like their voice shapes who gets recruited next.

"Vote for the subjects you most want covered. The top 10 guide who we recruit next. Become an Instructor if you can help."

— the page's actual headline copy

🏆

Leaderboard psychology

A live ranked list with vote counts (47, 31, 28…) creates visible competition. Supporters of a subject naturally want to push it higher — so they vote, share the link, and recruit friends to do the same. That word-of-mouth brings new sign-ups.

🙋

Direct instructor recruitment

"Become an Instructor if you can help" sits right below the leaderboard. Someone who teaches Piano and sees 47 votes demanding it gets an unmistakable signal that the market is waiting. The page converts skilled people who weren't considering teaching online.

📍

Location filter makes it local

Filtering to "Top 10 within 100 miles of Denver" turns a global wishlist into a neighbourhood conversation. Local requests feel urgent and personal — and local instructors feel directly called to respond.

👍

One-tap voting creates accounts

Clicking the thumbs-up on a request prompts sign-in if the user isn't logged in. The desire to support a cause they care about — Piano for their child, Mandarin for themselves — is enough motivation to create an account on the spot.

🔢

10-request cap creates thoughtful curation

The limit of 10 suggestions per user means people choose carefully and feel ownership of their picks. That investment makes them return to check if their subject moved up the ranks — driving repeat engagement.

Technical Architecture
Modern, secure, and built to scale
⚛️

React + TypeScript

Component-based SPA with strict typing. Vite build tooling, React Router 7 for navigation.

🗄️

Supabase

PostgreSQL database, Auth, Storage, and Realtime. Row Level Security on every table — the DB rejects unauthorised queries regardless of client behaviour.

🗺️

Leaflet Maps

Interactive instructor map with custom SVG price-tag pins. Gold pins for verified instructors, purple for group sessions. Spread algorithm prevents pin overlap.

🔒

Row Level Security

Every booking, message, and goal enforces ownership at the database level. Admin access checked via is_admin boolean — never client-side only.

⚡

Supabase Realtime

Live message delivery and notification counts via Postgres change subscriptions. No polling — messages appear instantly.

💳

Stripe Payments

Checkout integration fully implemented. Session payment flows activate with a live Stripe account — no further development needed.
